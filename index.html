How we’ll work

A clear path,
no surprises.

Every project follows the same six steps, so you always know what comes next and what you’ll receive at each stage.

Intro call & brief

We talk through your goals, site, budget and timeline, and agree the scope.

You get: a written brief and scope

Site survey

Measure, photograph and document existing conditions so the design starts from facts.

You get: a documented site survey

Concept & layouts

Options for the plan and the experience, tested against how the space will be used.

You get: layout options & concept visuals

Design development

The chosen layout is developed into detailed interiors, materials and 3D views.

You get: developed drawings & renders

Coordination

Working with structural consultants, vendors and contractors so drawings can be built.

You get: a coordinated set & programme

Site supervision & handover

Regular site visits, progress reports and quality checks through to completion.

You get: progress reports & a handover

FAQ

Good questions.

Where can you work?

I’m based in Buraidah, Al Qassim, with strong ties to Lahore. I can work on site in the Qassim region and in Pakistan, and remotely on design, drawings and 3D anywhere.

Are you a licensed architect?

Yes. I hold architect licence no. A011966 and a five-year Bachelor of Architecture from COMSATS University Islamabad (Lahore campus).

Can you handle more than one site at a time?

Yes. On the HAVAL GWM rollout I designed layouts for more than 15 showroom sites and managed on-site execution of several at once, using Gantt charts and supervision reports to keep each one on track.

What do you need from me to get started?

A short description of the project, the location, a rough timeline and any drawings or photos you already have. That’s enough for a first conversation; we’ll define the full scope together.

Do you work with other consultants and contractors?

Regularly. I’ve coordinated with structural consultants, vendors and contractors to make sure what’s built matches the design intent.
